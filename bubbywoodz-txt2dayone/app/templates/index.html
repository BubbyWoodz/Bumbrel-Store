📝 TXT → Day One Converter

Turn a zip of .txt/.md journal entries into a Day One-format zip, ready for Mneme's importer.

🔒 Everything runs on this server. Your files are wiped from temp storage the moment the converted zip is ready. Nothing is sent anywhere.

How it works
  1. Upload a .zip containing your .txt or .md journal entries.
  2. Dates are read from filenames — 2024-01-15.txt, 2024_01_15, Jan 15 2024, etc. If no date is found, the file's modified date is used.
  3. Download the Day One-format .zip.
  4. In Mneme, use the Day One import to bring in your entries. Everything is encrypted on your device.
